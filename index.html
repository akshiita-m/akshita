A little context

Many interests.
One person.

I’m Akshita Mahajan, from Indore, and I love gulab jamun. I once read that people forget names but remember details. If you remember one thing about me, let it be gulab jamun.

Painting and embroidery live alongside robotics and electronics in my life. I don’t see them as competing interests; each one teaches me to notice a different kind of detail.

Off the project list: Taylor Swift and Sabrina Carpenter on repeat, Gossip Girl and Modern Family, films, and books like Verity and Gone Girl. I’m drawn to stories with a sharp turn in them.

Also: baking, hot chocolate, and hot coffee without sugar. Yoga and cycling help me reset.

People tell me I speak in random facts. My room stays organised; it’s easier than organising my thoughts. I’m making this website a little chaotic, just like me.

For my formal résumé, see work & milestones.
