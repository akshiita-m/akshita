I joined Agriguru Technologies as a Hardware Engineer Intern. I learned the hardware side of the product while working with a team focused on agricultural problems.

03 · Community & sport

Practice that
travels.

YogaTeaching & competition

Yoga beyond the classroom

I chose yoga as my sport in high school. Over time, it became a practice in listening to my breath, noticing my body, and finding steadiness—on the mat and beyond it.

I represented my school at the School Games Federation of India (SGFI) Nationals and completed yoga teacher training during my junior year. I’ve also volunteered to teach at an old age home, an orphanage, and in my community.

Community initiativeDigitisation

A community directory, with less paper

I proposed putting our community’s directory online to save paper. It wasn’t only for matrimony: it also included women running small businesses, students at leading colleges, and other people and resources across the community.
